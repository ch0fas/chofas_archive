Mis Favs

Mis Favs is the canon of all my favorite songs.

The concept of Mis Favs is simple: every month I add 20 songs (a "class") that I can never get rid of. The rules to add new songs are as follows:

- Max three songs by the same artist/group per class, and all 3 songs have to be from different albums.
- Before being eligible for Mis Favs, a song must be a "candidate" for addition for at least three months, to avoid me adding a song just because I really like it right now.
