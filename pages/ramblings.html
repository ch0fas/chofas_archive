Apple FINALLY made a perfect watch face.

15/09/2026

Kinda buried-but-not-really-because-it-was-siri-related in Apple's event last week was a new Siri watch face for the Apple Watch, which makes it a lot quicker to call Siri if you want to.

The watch faces in question (Credit: 9to5Mac)

I will NOT be using it for AI because Siri AI is not supported on my iPhone. However, this is literally all I've ever wanted from an Apple Watch Face: the date, the time in big, beautiful serif font and 3 extra widgets. I just removed Siri and added the widgets I actually wanted. I love this so much.
